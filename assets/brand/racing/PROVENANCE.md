# Racing series wordmarks

F2 and F3 vector artwork comes from the corresponding championship header
logos, retrieved on 2026-11-12:

- https://www.fiaformula2.com/ — “FIA Formula2 Championship” SVG
- https://www.fiaformula3.com/ — “FIA Formula3 Championship” SVG

At the user’s request, the separate FIA championship lockup is omitted. The
F-number paths and their explicit blue/orange fills are unchanged; the viewBox
is cropped to those paths. The logos remain their respective owners’ marks.
F1 uses the default wordmark bundled with PaddockJS.
