Neural Noir

A detective game with generated cases and suspect dialogue. Explore a villa, gather evidence, and test your accusation against the case.

  • Solo project
  • Unreal Engine 5
  • Gemini · Python
View source ↗Download build · 34 GB ↗

Keeping interviews consistent with the case

Gemini generates suspects, motives, a timeline, and the solution. Characters and clues refer to that case throughout the investigation.

The final verdict also uses the case to assess your accusation. Dialogue can be open-ended while the underlying events remain fixed.
