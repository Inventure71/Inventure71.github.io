Narrative

A noir experience that never repeats

Neural Noir lets players play director and detective at once. Pick a theme—or paste in a wild prompt—and Gemini AI generates a villain, suspects, victim dossier, custom evidence props, and even the villa layout. Every loop pushes improvisation: characters respond using their own motives, props inherit bespoke lore, and your final accusation is evaluated against the canonical truth. Instead of writing one perfect murder mystery, I built a machine that writes thousands.

  • Unreal Engine 5
  • Gemini AI Streaming
  • Python Orchestrator
  • C++ Gameplay Systems

Systems

What makes the casework feel alive

AI-Generated Mysteries

Gemini AI stitches suspects, motives, and red herrings into a coherent timeline so no two cases unfold the same way.

Living Villa Sandbox

The manor fills with bespoke props and interactables that reference the current case file, giving physical weight to AI text.

Dynamic Cast

Each NPC keeps a memory stack of your interrogations, evolving their alibis and attitude based on how aggressive you get.

Forensic Loop

Victim dossiers and medical readouts are auto-written, letting you cross-reference wounds, tools, and timelines like a real detective.
