Neural Noir

A detective game where every murder is written from scratch by AI. Ask for any theme and Gemini invents the suspects, the motives, the clues, and the culprit — so no two cases are ever the same, and neither am I when I get one wrong.

The short version

A procedural detective sandbox. Ask for any theme and it builds you a bespoke crime scene — suspects, alibis, a weapon, a solution — in seconds. Then you interrogate, gather evidence, and make your accusation.

  • Spins up a whole cast of suspects, alibis, and props on demand.
  • Gemini holds the noir tone steady while bending the twists around your choices.
  • An AI judge grades your final accusation — and shows its receipts.

Systems

What makes the casework feel alive

A mystery written on the spot

Gemini scripts the suspects, motives, and timeline for each case, blending your prompt with classic noir.

  • Constraints keep the motives logical instead of random.
  • Red herrings stay believable — but the case is always solvable.

A villa that fits the case

Props, rooms, and clues are populated to match whatever crime just got generated, so the evidence feels like it belongs there.

  • Interactables get their lore straight from the generated case.
  • The scene reacts as you poke at clues.

Suspects who remember

NPCs hold onto what you've asked them, shifting between cooperative and cagey depending on how you press.

  • Your tone changes theirs — lean on them and they clam up.
  • Interrogations dig into secrets that surface mid-case.

The evidence has to add up

Auto-written dossiers link wounds, timing, and the weapon, so your accusation stands or falls on the actual clues.

  • Photographing and tagging evidence feeds the case file.
  • The verdict grades your logic, not just your final guess.

Even losing makes a good story

The verdict scene remixes the clues you found, quotes the suspects back at you, and calls out the contradictions you missed. Get it wrong and you don't just see "incorrect" — you get a little piece of noir narration explaining exactly how you were played.

Flow

How a case unfolds

01

Pick a theme

You choose a genre and tone, and Gemini quietly decides the truth of the case — who did it, and why.

02

Build the scene

Characters, props, and clues spawn across the villa, each carrying a story that fits the tone you asked for.

03

Investigate

Interview suspects, photograph evidence, and cross-check the medical notes to catch people in their contradictions.

04

Accuse

Name your culprit and get back a Gemini-written verdict that grades your logic, your accuracy, and the evidence you leaned on.
