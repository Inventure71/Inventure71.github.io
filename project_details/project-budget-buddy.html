Budget Buddy

A hackathon prototype for discussing spending, savings goals, and budget changes in a conversation.

Python LLM Hackathon
Role Hackathon team
Timeline Hackathon build
Stack Python · LLM
Status Weekend prototype

Spending and goals

The assistant uses transaction categories and previous conversations to discuss budget changes and savings goals.

  • Breaks down the spending relevant to your question.
  • Compares proposed changes with your savings goals.

What we built

Transaction data and conversation history

Transaction categories

Combines transactions and standardizes merchant names for the spending summary.

  • Groups spending into categories.
  • Flags unusual weekly spending.

Conversation history

Keeps earlier goals and decisions available for follow-up conversations.

  • Connects earlier decisions with transaction history.
  • Uses previous goals when proposing a change.

Model inputs

The prototype masks sensitive details before sending model requests.

  • The raw numbers stay local; only what's needed goes out.

The hackathon focus

The prototype explored how a conversation could help someone compare a purchase or savings goal against their spending. The focus was the interaction: explaining a suggested budget change and keeping track of the decisions made.

Flow

How a chat plays out

01

Check in

You tell it what's on your mind — a big purchase, a rough month, a goal — in plain language.

02

Review spending

It retrieves spending related to your question.

03

Talk it through

It proposes a budget change and explains how it relates to your goal.

04

Follow up

The next conversation can refer to your earlier decisions.
