Guardrails

How the coaching brain stays trustworthy

Ledger sync core

Unifies Plaid feeds and CSV uploads into a reconciled ledger with anomaly flags and audit notes.

  • Automatic category repair when institutions rename merchants.
  • Statement variance detection keeps weekly overviews honest.

Conversation memory

Captures emotion, commitments, and follow-up intents so the copilot mirrors previous agreements.

  • Timeline view stitches chat highlights with ledger events.
  • Promise tracker reminds the AI before it gives new advice.

Compliance wrapper

Policy sandbox masks personally identifiable information before prompts leave the secure enclave.

  • Prompt and response journaling with immutable hashes.
  • Role-based approvals when exporting conversational summaries.

Budget Buddy handles the tough talks

The copilot cross-references goals with cash flow and gently reframes the conversation when the numbers disagree. Instead of shaming, it proposes trade-offs and schedules future treats once the ledger supports them.
