Project Snapshot

Built to retire spreadsheets and manual reconciliations, DrSorrisoDonations keeps donation flows auditable while delighting donors with timely updates.

  • Real-time campaign dashboard with retention, average gift, and conversion KPIs.
  • Automated thank-you and compliance receipts tailored by donor segment.

Impact loops

How the platform keeps donors close

  • Campaign health

    Dashboards tie pledges, disbursements, and stories together so coordinators see which programs need fresh attention.

  • Volunteer follow-ups

    Weekly digests surface lapsed donors with context, scripts, and the last touchpoint to make outreach effortless.

  • Compliance guardrails

    Consent flags, GDPR exports, and receipt archives live in the same workflow, keeping the legal team confident.

Operating spine

How donors and staff stay aligned

Unified intake

Gateway adapters validate signatures, normalize currencies, and attach campaign metadata before storage.

  • Stripe and PayPal payloads collapse into a shared schema.
  • Manual gifts imported via spreadsheet use the same pipeline.

Donor relationship map

CRM layer tracks consent flags, communication preferences, and volunteer notes for each supporter.

  • Lapsed donor alerts feed weekly volunteer stand-ups.
  • Automations adapt tone based on donor lifecycle stage.

Transparent allocations

Ledger splits each donation across programs so finance, marketing, and leadership share the same numbers.

  • Impact stories pull directly from allocation metadata.
  • Audit logs mirror every manual override or refund.

Volunteers see who needs a thank-you next

Weekly digests pair donor sentiment with giving history so community teams know which families to call, who to celebrate, and when to flag finance about unusual activity.

Flow

How a donation travels

01
Capture

A supporter completes a web form; payment gateway webhook verifies and delivers event payload.

02
Process

Donation service links the gift to a donor record, applies campaign attribution, and records allocation intent.

03
Acknowledge

Background worker sends personalized thank-you, issues receipt PDF, and updates stakeholder dashboards.

04
Report

Nightly analytics snapshots refresh retention metrics, segment changes, and impact exports for finance.
