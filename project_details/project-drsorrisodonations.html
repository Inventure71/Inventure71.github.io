Narrative

Why centralize the donation journey

Dr Sorriso’s fundraising data lived across payment gateways, inboxes, and offline spreadsheets, making it hard to share timely impact. I designed DrSorrisoDonations to ingest payments, normalize donor profiles, and orchestrate follow-up messaging in one secure backend. The service exposes APIs for web forms and third-party apps, stores a transparent allocation ledger, and surfaces insights that help volunteers act faster—from identifying lapsed donors to celebrating campaign milestones.

  • FastAPI + PostgreSQL service core
  • Stripe & PayPal gateway adapters
  • Celery workers for emails & reports
  • Pandas-powered analytics snapshots

Systems

What keeps donors confident

Unified Intake

REST endpoints and webhooks capture one-off and recurring gifts, verifying signatures before committing transactions.

Donor CRM

Profiles track consent flags, preferences, and lifecycle segments to drive personalized outreach.

Allocation Ledger

Every donation can be split across programs with auditable metadata for impact storytelling.

Automated Touchpoints

Template engine triggers thank-you emails, milestone updates, and tax receipts with merge fields.

Analytics Hub

Daily jobs roll campaign performance, retention trends, and source attribution into sharable dashboards.

Compliance Layer

Role-based access, audit events, and secure PII storage satisfy reporting and privacy expectations.

Flow

How a donation travels

01
Capture

A supporter completes a web form; payment gateway webhook verifies and delivers event payload.

02
Process

Donation service links the gift to a donor record, applies campaign attribution, and records allocation intent.

03
Acknowledge

Background worker sends personalized thank-you, issues receipt PDF, and updates stakeholder dashboards.

04
Report

Nightly analytics snapshots refresh retention metrics, segment changes, and impact exports for finance.
