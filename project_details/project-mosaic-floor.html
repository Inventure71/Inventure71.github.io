From an observation to the floor

  1. ObserveRead selected robot telemetry and independent tracking data.
  2. CheckRetain the original timestamp, coordinate frame and validity of each observation.
  3. ProjectMap observations through shared calibration into the floor display.

Keep the meaning of the measurement

A value can arrive successfully and still be too old to use. Typed contracts retain source times, units, frames and provenance across the hub and display. Missing or stale evidence is shown as unavailable.

Shared calibration
The hub owns the mapping between tracking coordinates and the projection surface.
Independent observations
External tracking and robot-reported reasoning remain distinguishable in the display.
Separate responsibilities
The visualization observes the robot application. Movement and stopping authority remain with MOSAIC’s robot-local agents.

Record a run, inspect it again

Versioned recordings

Recordings preserve typed observations and metadata so a debugging session can be revisited with its timing context intact.

Observation-only replay

Replay feeds the display and checks without sending movement commands to live robots. The same evidence can be examined away from the original run.

One project, separate responsibilities

This component focuses on floor debugging and projection within MOSAIC. The fleet controller coordinates robot movement, while visualization and replay remain observation-only. Physical deployment of the display requires projector alignment, tracking calibration and checks on the lab hardware.
