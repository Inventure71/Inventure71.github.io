Narrative

Why merge games, cells, and robots

I wanted one sandbox where perception, reasoning, and actuation could iterate together. VictorIA began as a Connect 4 agent, then expanded when I needed tooling for annotating cell imagery and prototyping robotic responses. Rather than scatter scripts across repositories, I invested in shared modules, data contracts, and orchestration so each experiment can borrow assets from the others—letting a vision experiment influence the next robotics trial or strategy sim.

  • Python 3.x
  • OpenCV Visualization
  • NumPy & SciPy heuristics
  • Serial / hardware adapters

Systems

What powers the sandbox

Connect 4 Brain

connect4.py models the board, simulates futures with minimax, and exposes a clean API for human or automated play.

Vision Lab Tools

ImagePointSelection.py drives an annotation loop that writes selections into Processed_Cells/ for downstream models.

Robot Testbed

RobotExperimental.py converts game states or image-derived targets into device commands for physical or simulated arms.

Shared Utilities

Support modules inside utils/ keep logging, math helpers, and file I/O consistent, cutting duplication across experiments.

Flow

How the platform runs a scenario

01
Mode Selection

Launch main.py and pick a lane—game AI duel, image point lab, or robot exercise.

02
Data Capture

Load boards, ingest imagery, or read hardware sensors; normalize everything through utils/ adapters.

03
Analysis & Decision

Strategy modules, detectors, or heuristics transform the state into actionable commands or annotations.

04
Actuation & Export

Robotics routines move pieces, processed images land in Processed_Cells/, and logs capture performance metrics.
