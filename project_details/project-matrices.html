An unrated movie is not a zero-star movie

The example starts with ten users and sixteen movies. Only observed ratings contribute to each movie’s average. Known ratings become residuals around that baseline; a missing entry means no observed deviation, rather than a negative review.

Sparse input
Keep the known user–movie ratings as a coordinate list.

Compute the average rating from the available observations.
Residuals
Represent how each observed rating differs from its movie’s baseline.
