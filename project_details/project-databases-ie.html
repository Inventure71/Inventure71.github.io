Data design

Exact records, guarded transitions

Normalize the card

Games, sets, cards, exact variants, and images are distinct records. Inventory, listings, order lines, and price snapshots all point to the exact variant they describe.

Make invalid states difficult

Database constraints reject negative quantities, over-reserved stock, duplicate printings, invalid listing amounts, and buyer-equals-seller orders before those states can spread.

Move stock atomically

Marketplace services use transactions and select_for_update() so creating, cancelling, or purchasing a listing changes inventory and price history as one consistent operation.
