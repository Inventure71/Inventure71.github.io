Quick Type

Choose where to type.

Prepare the text, choose a profile, and select a destination.

  1. Prepare your text

    Write or paste into Quick Type, then choose a saved typing profile.

  2. Choose the destination

    Press Start, then ⇧ Shift ⌥ Option-click the field where it should go.

  3. Keep control

    Your keyboard or mouse input pauses typing. Resume by choosing where to continue, or stop from the panel, menu bar, or shortcut.
