Typing for macOS

Ghoststroke

Turn prepared text into typing, with your own pace, pauses, and corrections. Choose a destination field and start from a compact macOS panel.

Swift 5.9SwiftUI / AppKitAccessibility + Core Graphics
Role
Creator · macOS engineer
Platform
Apple silicon · macOS 14+

Quick Type

Paste or write your text, choose a saved cadence, and press Start. Shift-Option-click selects the destination field.

  • Select the destination before typing starts.
  • Pause, resume, or stop from the panel, menu bar, or shortcut.

Quick Type

From text to typing

01

Prepare in Quick Type

Enter the exact source, select a built-in or saved profile, and decide whether line breaks become spaces, Return, or Shift-Return.

02

Choose a field

Start target selection, then Shift-Option-click the destination. Typing starts at the cursor you select, including in canvas-based editors. Recognized password fields are rejected.

03

Start typing

The typing sequence can include pauses and temporary mistakes that are corrected back to your prepared text.

04

Pause without starting over

Continue from where you paused, or choose another destination. Stop from the panel, menu bar, or shortcut.

Studio

Adjust the typing rhythm

  • Pace and corrections

    Adjust speed, pauses, and how often typing mistakes occur. Preview the sequence before sending it to another app.

  • Generated false starts

    An optional local model prepares temporary phrases that are typed, erased, and followed by your original text. It unloads after preparation.

  • Test Bench

    Try a profile in a typing canvas, see upcoming pauses and corrections, and replay a phrase.

  • Profiles, drafts, recovery

    Save custom profiles and keep composer drafts between sessions. Built-in profiles remain available.

  • Setup

    Configure Accessibility access, shortcuts, line breaks, and whether the app appears in the Dock.

Availability and compatibility

A public download is not available yet. Ghoststroke requires Accessibility permission; some apps reject simulated typing.
